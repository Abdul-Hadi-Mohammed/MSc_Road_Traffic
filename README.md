# RB1 Sustainable Mobility Project: Rail Energy Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This report evaluates the transition of the **Zwickau Zentrum – Kraslice (RB1)** rail route from traditional diesel power to sustainable, zero-emission drive systems. Spanning 70.58 km of non-electrified track, this route serves as a critical case study for implementing modern rail technology in regional German transport.

The study assesses the operational feasibility, infrastructure requirements, and total cost of ownership for two primary alternatives: **Battery Electric Multiple Units (BEMU)** and **Hydrogen Electric Multiple Units (HEMU)**.

## 🚆 Route Specifications
* **Route Name:** RB1 (Vogtlandbahn).
* **Connection:** Zwickau Zentrum ↔ Kraslice (via Falkenstein).
* **Total Distance:** 70.58 km.
* **Electrification:** 0% (The entire route is currently non-electrified).
* **Current Operations:** Exclusively diesel-powered RegioSprinter units.

## ⚙️ Alternative Drive Systems Comparison

| Characteristic | Battery-Electric (BEMU) | Hydrogen Fuel Cell (HEMU) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Emissions** | Zero Local Emissions | Zero (Water Vapor only) |
| **Energy Efficiency** | High (3.9 kWh/km) | Moderate (5.9 kWh/km) |
| **Operational Range** | 40–150 km | Up to 600 km |
| **Refueling Time** | 10 mins (Station Charging) | 15 mins (Hydrogen Tank) |
| **Infrastructure** | Charging stations / Island OHL | Hydrogen refueling stations |

## 💰 Economic Conclusion
The financial analysis identifies **BEMU** as the superior choice for the RB1 route due to lower procurement costs and significantly lower annual energy expenses.

| Investment Category | BEMU (3 Units) | HEMU (3 Units) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vehicle Procurement** | €18,300,000 | €20,100,000 |
| **Infrastructure Setup** | €6,000,000 | €10,500,000 |
| **Annual Energy Cost** | ~€4,630,000 | ~€13,370,000 |

## 🛠 Project Methodology
1. **Route Analysis:** Assessment of track length, station layout, and optimal charging locations at Zwickau Zentrum, Falkenstein, and Kraslice.
2. **Energy Modeling:** Calculation of energy consumed per trip based on manufacturer specs and real-world gradients.
3. **Operational Feasibility:** Evaluation of turnaround times and charging durations to ensure no disruption to the existing schedule.
4. **Cost-Benefit Analysis:** Comparison of initial investment versus long-term maintenance and lifecycle costs.
